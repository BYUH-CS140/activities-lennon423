Statue of Liberty

Statue of Liberty, a world-famous symbol of freedom and democracy, stands proudly in New York Harbor. Gifted by France in 1886, it was designed by Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi with a framework by Gustave Eiffel. Representing Libertas, the Roman goddess of liberty, it has long welcomed immigrants and visitors to the United States.

We finally saw it with our own eyes—the iconic symbol of New York City. It was a memorable experience, made even better by sharing it with my friends from the choir.
