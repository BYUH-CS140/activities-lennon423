Biography

Hi, I’m John Lennon Onda, the heart behind the lens at JLEO Studio.

My journey into photography began with a simple wish — to capture the moments I never had the chance to keep. Growing up, we didn’t have many photos to look back on, and that absence made me realize how powerful memories become when frozen in time.

That’s what drives me today: the desire to help others preserve their most precious memories. Whether it’s a quiet smile, a joyful celebration, or a fleeting glance — I believe every photo tells a story worth keeping.

Let’s create something beautiful that you can always look back on.
